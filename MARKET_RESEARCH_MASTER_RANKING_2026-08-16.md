## Ranking rule

The ranking intentionally does **not** force every business model into one PE formula.

```text
non-cyclical / quality compounders -> normalized equity profit + quality-adjusted PE
commodity / memory cyclicals       -> forward-cycle profit + through-cycle normalized profit
AI hardware / long duration        -> CURRENT_FORWARD_PE vs discounted TERMINAL_PE expectation test
A/H dual-listed companies          -> class-specific market-cap caveat; A-price-implied total equity is not actual consolidated market cap
low-quality headline earnings      -> recurring/core operating profit, not headline profit
```

The master question is:

> How much sustainable future profit is the current price already demanding, and is that requirement lower than, close to, or above a credible earnings path?
