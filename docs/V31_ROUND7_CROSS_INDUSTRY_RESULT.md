# V3.1 Round-7 cross-industry OOS result

Status: **promising cross-industry evidence; research only; production V3.1 unchanged**.

Round 7 reused the frozen Round-6 economic formula on eight untouched Shanghai/Shenzhen main-board securities spanning high-end manufacturing, automation, components, industrial software, a consumer platform, resources, grid equipment and mature consumer cash flow. The formula, universe and parameters were committed before the result workflow was introduced.

The model run completed in GitHub Actions run `32928879454`. The original persistence step lost a push race to two concurrent repository automations, but the uploaded artifact was intact. This was a Git transport failure after the backtest, not a model or data failure.

## Comparator diagnosis

Versus Round 5, Expectation Gap added RMB 1.522m of ending capital and 10.13 percentage points of CAGR. Average cash fell by 26.33 percentage points. Maximum drawdown was 3.77 percentage points deeper, but Sharpe improved by 0.637.

Versus Universal PE/PB, Expectation Gap added RMB 514,660 of ending capital and 2.69 percentage points of CAGR while improving maximum drawdown by 12.35 percentage points and Sharpe by 0.399. It held 8.11 percentage points more cash, but the higher return means the improvement cannot be explained only by de-risking into cash.

Versus true buy-and-hold, Expectation Gap lagged by RMB 472,891 and 2.10 percentage points of CAGR. In exchange, maximum drawdown improved by 31.83 percentage points and Sharpe improved by 0.489. On this fixed universe the frozen model delivered materially better risk-adjusted performance, although not the highest terminal wealth.

This is a substantially stronger result than Round 6. It is evidence that the ten-year expectation engine can work across several non-identical industries; it is not evidence that the engine is universally stable.

## Growth and implied-expectation diagnosis

The Round-6 weakness in backward-looking supportable growth persisted:

- 埃斯顿's realistic growth was floored at 0% on 59.3% of all test days;
- 分众传媒 was floored at 0% on 58.2%;
- 特变电工 was floored at 0% on 40.5%;
- 宝信软件 was floored at 0% on 31.1%;
- 恒立液压 hit the 30% cap on 46.6% of days.

The floor and cap are economically binding, not rare safety guards. This confirms that a single approximately three-year backward CAGR is an unstable point estimate across cycles.

埃斯顿 is the clearest failure. It had zero BUY-eligible days. On 319 days, including the final observation, its price exceeded even the value generated by a 100% starting-growth rate. Latest normalized inputs produced a neutral value of only RMB 0.33 against a RMB 30.82 price. The solver correctly returned `IMPLIED_ABOVE_SEARCH_RANGE`; the implausible scale is evidence that the normalized earning-power/growth input is not representing this business well, not permission to expand the search range.

At the other extreme, 特变电工 spent 1,235 days below the zero-growth value and 1,218 days at BUY_STAGED or better. The model can therefore swing between extremely cheap and effectively impossible implied expectations depending on company/cycle state.

## SELL diagnosis

Round 7 produced 83 genuine negative-weight valuation SELL events. Repeated monthly observations are correlated.

| Horizon | Complete events | Median forward return | Positive fraction | Median maximum upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 months | 82 | 2.55% | 53.7% | 25.59% |
| 12 months | 69 | 2.85% | 53.6% | 37.84% |
| 24 months | 62 | 16.62% | 61.3% | 80.25% |

The first SELL after an intervening BUY produced 14 sell-regime entries. Of the 11 with a complete 24-month window, 81.8% later had positive returns; median 24-month return was 26.62% and median maximum upside was 116.78%.

This is less severe at the raw 12-month event level than Round 6, but the de-correlated 24-month evidence still shows material early selling. SELL retains risk-control value, yet the neutral value does not reliably track multi-year upside.

## Integrity audit

The saved audit found zero:

- `available_date < report_date` errors;
- deviations from the later profit/cash-flow `NOTICE_DATE`;
- future financial merges;
- duplicate daily dates;
- BUY trades with negative weight change;
- valuation SELL trades with positive weight change.

Buy-and-hold now retains an explicit original-capital observation, so the first 0.10% cost no longer cancels during metric normalization.

## Decision

Round 7 materially improves the evidence base but does not justify production promotion:

- one of eight fresh companies was still permanently unowned;
- the growth floor/cap binds too often;
- implied growth can become economically unusable when normalized EPS is depressed;
- sell-regime entries still precede substantial 24-month upside;
- the test remains a fixed-universe valuation/execution study without historical qualitative hard-gate reconstruction.

Do not tune Round 7. Preserve it as positive and negative OOS evidence. A later formula change should focus on a cycle-aware range for supportable growth and explicit earning-power/reinvestment evidence, and must use another untouched universe. Production `selection_framework_v31.py` remains unchanged.
