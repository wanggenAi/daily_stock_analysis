## 1. Non-negotiable user outcome

The user is the investment decision maker. The system must do its complex upstream collection, official-source checks, calculation, valuation, Jev advisory classification, research, market, industry, cycle and event triage **in the background**, then convert the *verified output* into a living, low-noise report. Home should answer immediately:

1. What do I do with each confirmed stock/fund position today, and **what actually changed**?
2. Which newly researched stocks have a genuinely actionable, presently valid **formal** BUY/ADD/WAIT_PRICE plan, ranked by actionable attractiveness and risk-adjusted fit, with explicit price, quantity and invalidation conditions?
3. What secular social/AI/technology/market trends and cyclical troughs are supported by observable demand, industry profit pools and causal corporate beneficiaries? Drill from trend -> bottleneck -> company -> independently underwritten value and entry.
4. How much cash can be deployed **under existing authority**, what should be reserved, and what stays uninvested?
5. Did the decisions and prices lead to measurable realized/forward value, or did their rationale fail?

Do not force the owner to study missing gates, workflow IDs, Deep statuses, Jev decisions, mathematical models or research-process repetition on the home page. These remain fully auditable in drill-down/engineering views. **Do not force a BUY to satisfy the output goal.** When there is no validated opportunity, show a clear zero-action/cash-preservation result and the reason in plain language; fabricate neither evidence nor authorization.

## 2. Home layout: a compact decision cockpit

**Above fold (one-screen digest):** Beijing date/time + exact market/data-as-of timestamps, freshness per feed (EOD vs intraday vs filings vs broker), MARKET gate, position exposure and cash, count of *new* formal changes, real immediately executable actions, qualified opportunity count, and 1–3 materially changed events. Differentiate `new signal`, `persistent unchanged authorization`, `consumed authority` and `research-only`.

**Panel A — 我的持仓 / confirmed portfolio, first:** confirmed equities **and confirmed funds**; code/name, actual shares/units, user-confirmed cost and evidence date, **clearly timestamped reference/last valid live price**, cost/P&L, value interval and confidence, exposure/concentration, official-sourced material events and impact, formal persisted action/lifecycle, executable lot-size/T+1 constraints, permitted position-size change, two-stage buy or reduce/exit conditions, invalidation/next re-evaluation. Sort by *material action/change and portfolio risk*, not endless repeated research gaps. Expand any holding to issuer filings, meeting agenda/resolutions (proposals must NOT be presented as passed resolutions), annual/interim financials, cash-flow, order/demand, policy, counterevidence, valuation derivation and exact source/as-of. Portfolio data can be updated only from new user-confirmed broker evidence; when funds are unconfirmed show `latest holdings not persisted`, not zero holdings.

**Panel B — 机会榜 / actionable opportunities:** display a **small, honest set** of already qualified formal, current, provenance-verified candidates only; BUY-now, eligible WAIT_PRICE, and hold/add are separate states. Each row: code, sector/company name, why this company (replicable ASML-style moat/bottleneck, resilient multi-year demand, real earnings/cash, financial safety), price as-of/source, fair-value **range with assumptions and confidence**, buy ceiling, upside/downside or reward/risk, trigger, lot, maximum proposed exposure, planned sell/partial-take-profit and fundamental thesis-invalidation conditions; explain the ranking in plain investor language. Ranking is only among actually comparable, qualified opportunities; never rank weak/incomplete leads as ready-to-buy. `Research BUY`, Jev `ENTRY_NOW` and advisory `BUILD/PROBE` **are NOT** Formal BUY and never receive executable BUY/ADD buttons without matching current canonical authority, market gate, fresh executable quote and cash/position limits. Inaction should be prominent when no valid buy exists.

**Panel C — 趋势雷达 / structural & emerging:** show at most the most decision-relevant emerging/accelerating/confirmed secular trends, plus crowding, weakening and contradictory evidence. Explore GDP/industrial production/consumption/investment where contextually relevant; official policy/regulatory texts and implementation (not headline-only), verified demand/order/penetration, industry capex, technology cost curve, competition, financial attention vs true financing and earnings capture. Track independent 10–20y structural, 3–10y industrial and 6–36mo cyclical horizons; AI inflection is a *hypothesis to verify*, not an automatic winner. Drill into supply-chain bottlenecks, profit pools, eligible A-share listed firms, then pass existing V3.1.1 Deep/valuation/quality/authority review. Trend scores never create trading authority.

**Panel D — 周期雷达 / independently validated cyclical troughs:** eligible commodities, components, manufacturers and cyclical businesses with a *verified*, company-specific historical/current cycle position. Show supply/demand/inventory/prices/utilization, capacity exits, relevant policy, cost curve, balance-sheet survival, normalized earnings, company durable moat, historical vs today's cycle and valuation, reversal catalysts, falsifiers and risk of a structural decline masquerading as a trough. Avoid 'must rise' or 'certain rebound' claims. Large allocations require independent validation of expected return, drawdown, liquidity, diversification, loss budget and the existing current authority/capital rules; no automatic oversized positions.

**Panel E — 今日真正发生的变化 and performance:** daily diff of materially new official announcements, shareholder meeting **proposals vs actual outcomes**, earnings/orders, policy effective dates, cycle/trend upgrades/downgrades, price moving into/out of **authorized** range and user-confirmed execution consumption. Show thesis-performance/outcome ledger (realized P&L only with validated fills; prospective returns clearly marked, benchmark and horizon specified, no look-ahead). Each material changed assertion carries original URL, publication/effective date, observation time, corporate scope and a link to the full drill-down. Unchanged evidence should not create repeated notifications.

**Panel F — cash and daily final action table:** show confirmed/planning cash source/date, allocated vs unallocated funds, exposure/concentration constraints, permitted trades (ticker, action, lot, limit/trigger, amount, execution feasibility/time and 'needs manual confirmation'), deferred actions and no-action explanation. Never turn a target percentage into an invalid or rounded-up order.

The top page is a **projection** rather than source of truth: favor short actionable sentences, small tables and conditional visibility. Do not dump exhaustive reject pools, gate diagnostics, stale examples, internal CI/SHA/artifact IDs or verbose audit narratives into it. Put those behind stock/trend detail, operations, and evidence audit tabs. Preserve JSON and original official-source links for drill-down.

## 3. Research lifecycle: the living pool, not an endless gap loop

* Prioritize **existing confirmed holdings first** when impactful events, adverse fundamentals, risk triggers or stale executable quotes appear. Do not ignore funds once user confirms current fund positions.
* Broad radar continues independent diversity scanning, not hardcoded hot themes. For potential Runbei-like or ASML-like companies, demand independently supported multi-year moat, durable demand, earnings authenticity/cash conversion, financial safety, predictability, price discipline, out-of-sample strategy/exit validation and future-dated-information exclusion. A historic success example is a *comparison archetype*, not proof for new candidates.
* Classify leads as `DISCOVERED -> EVIDENCE_RESEARCH -> VALUATION_REVIEW -> FORMAL_ELIGIBLE / PARKED / REJECTED_OR_INVALIDATED` as **new presentation/research metadata**, NOT a replacement for current canonical or terminal enums. Existing deterministic gates and formal action sources stay authoritative.
* Set a bounded evidence budget and reason-coded terminal disposition. Missing **material** original evidence after the bounded attempt = `PARKED_EVIDENCE_EXHAUSTED`, removed from the owner-facing qualified list. A known hard contradiction = rejected/invalidated with reason; it is not the same as absence. Retain durable provenance and reopen only if the **source fingerprint/new issuer period/material independent event** changes; do not replay consumed Every-Industry/Deep/Jev merely to produce another gap report. Maintain a separate auditor-visible parked count, never silently promote UNKNOWN to PASS.
* Break the search -> report cycle: instead of showing 'promising but missing proof' as a featured pick, spend background research budget within limits; otherwise hide from the qualified home list until a genuine new evidence epoch. **Zero eligible stocks is an acceptable truthful report**, but measure full-universe/source coverage and independently fix broken collection/exit validation so false negatives do not persist indefinitely.
* A trend or cycle hypothesis remains *research only* until independent corporate underwriting and valid entry price are proven. Separate the probability/uncertainty of long-run thesis, current company fundamentals, and timing.
